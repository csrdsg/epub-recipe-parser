## 1. Roasted Carrots with Lemon

**Author:** EPUB Recipe Parser Contributors

**Book:** Small Kitchen Cookbook

**Chapter:** Vegetable Sides

**Serves: 2 | Prep Time: 10**

### Ingredients

- 4 carrots, sliced lengthwise
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- Salt to taste

### Instructions

1. Toss the carrots with the oil and salt and arrange them on a sheet pan.

2. Roast at 425°F for 20–25 minutes, turning once.

3. Drizzle with lemon juice before serving.

## 2. Ginger Tofu

**Author:** EPUB Recipe Parser Contributors

**Book:** Small Kitchen Cookbook

**Chapter:** Weeknight Mains

**Serves: 2**

### Ingredients

- 1 pound firm tofu, cut into cubes
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon grated ginger
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil

### Instructions

Combine the tofu, soy sauce, ginger, and oil in a bowl.
      Marinate for 30 minutes.

Spread the cubes on a sheet pan and bake at 400°F
      for 25 minutes. Let the tofu rest for 5 minutes before serving.
